# Privacy notes

- No account or sign-in is implemented.
- Favorites, playback progress and settings are stored locally with Room and Preferences DataStore.
- The app requests Internet access to load catalogue metadata and authorized media files.
- The app does not request location, contacts, microphone, phone, camera or storage permissions.
- The app does not currently include advertising or analytics SDKs.
- The Internet Archive and TVmaze receive network requests when their catalogues are used. Their own privacy policies and server logs are outside the app's control.
- Local favorites and playback progress are removed when the app's data is cleared or the app is uninstalled.
